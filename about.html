THE STORY

Objects should
mean something.

Washington Noir was imagined around a simple belief: what we choose to keep close should be made with intention.

Our approach brings together clean forms, thoughtful materials and details that reveal themselves over time. From a ring worn every day to a watch built around an individual point of view, every piece begins with the same question: does it feel considered?

We favour enduring design over passing trends, and personal character over excess. The result is a collection made to be lived with — and made more meaningful by the person who wears it.
